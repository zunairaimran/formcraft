Why FormCraft

Collecting data is easy. Choosing from it is not.

Picture a university event on 25 December with 50 seats and 200 interested students. FormCraft collects their details and then helps you select the eligible ones fairly.

Without FormCraft

  • Names, roll numbers and skills arrive on paper or in WhatsApp messages
  • Somebody copies everything into a sheet by hand
  • Shortlisting by semester or skill means searching row by row
  • Deciding who gets the limited seats is slow and easy to get wrong

With FormCraft

  • One link or QR code, and every answer is saved in a database
  • Answers are checked as people type, so the data stays clean
  • Filter by semester, skill, CGPA or anything you asked
  • Enter the venue capacity and the seats are allocated in one click

        FAQ
        
Questions people ask

      

      

        

          Is FormCraft free?
          
Yes. Create an account and make as many forms as you like.

        

        

          Do people need an account to fill in my form?
          
No. Anyone with the link or QR code can fill it in on a phone or a computer.

        

        

          Where are the answers stored?
          
In a SQL database on the server. Each answer is linked to its form, and only the form's owner can see the responses and files.

        

        

          How does seat allocation decide who gets a seat?
          
Responses that match your filters are eligible. The earliest eligible responses get the seats, the rest go on the waiting list, and you can still change any status by hand.

        

        

          Can I stop a form from getting too many answers?
          
Yes. Close it with one switch, or set a response limit and it closes by itself.

        

        

          Can I get the data into Excel?
          
Yes. Download the responses as a CSV file, which opens in Excel, or print the list.
